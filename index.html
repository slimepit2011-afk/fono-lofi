Application Purpose (應用程式用途)

FONO lofi (https://fono-lofi.vercel.app/) is a free web-based ambient music and study companion designed to enhance concentration, relaxation, and deep work. All users can freely browse and stream curated instrumental lo-fi beats, ambient audio soundscapes (rain, cafe background, warm vinyl crackle), and use Pomodoro focus timers directly in their browser without registering or logging in.

Optional Google OAuth: Signing in via Google OAuth is 100% voluntary. It is solely used to associate a user's identity (email and display name) so they can sync personalized playlists and uploaded tracks to their private vault across different browsers and devices.

Google APIs Terms of Service & Strict Anti-AI NCII Compliance

FONO lofi strictly complies with the Google APIs Terms of Service and the Google API Services User Data Policy.

  • No Generative Image / AI NCII: FONO lofi does not generate, synthesize, host, or distribute AI-generated Non-Consensual Intimate Imagery (NCII), deepfake pornography, or synthetic depictions of persons. FONO lofi provides zero facial modification or generative image services.
  • Audio Only: Any algorithmic playlist tools operate strictly on musical metadata (genre, tempo, mood tags) for ambient audio playback.
  • No Data Brokering: Google user credentials (openid, email, profile) are never sold, rented, or transferred to third parties or advertising brokers.
