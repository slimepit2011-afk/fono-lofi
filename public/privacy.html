Privacy Policy · 隱私權政策

Effective Date: November 22, 2026 · Application Name: FONO lofi · Official Homepage: https://fono-lofi.vercel.app/

✓ Open Access · No Login Required (完全公開 · 無需登入即可使用)

Welcome to FONO lofi ("we", "our", or "us"). FONO lofi is a free ambient focus and study music player that provides curated instrumental lo-fi beats, ambient audio soundscapes, and personal listening vaults.

Ungated Access: You can use FONO lofi, browse all music, play tracks, and use focus timers completely free without creating an account or logging in. Logging in with Google is 100% voluntary and is only used if you wish to sync custom uploaded audio files and personalized playlists across multiple devices.

1. Google User Data: Requested Scopes & Exact Purpose (Google 使用者資料收集與用途)

When you voluntarily choose to sign in with your Google Account, FONO lofi requests the minimum necessary scopes to authenticate you and establish your session. Below is the comprehensive breakdown:

Google API Scope Data Accessed (讀取資料) Exact Purpose & How It Is Used (明確用途說明)
openid Unique Google User ID (sub) Used securely via OpenID Connect to authenticate your session and associate your private music room.
.../auth/userinfo.email Email Address (電子郵件地址) Used solely as your primary account identifier so your custom playlists, liked tracks, and audio uploads belong exclusively to you.
.../auth/userinfo.profile Display Name & Profile Picture (姓名與頭像) Used solely to personalize your listening room header (e.g., displaying your greeting name and avatar). Never published or shared.
Google API Services User Data Policy / Limited Use Disclosure (限制使用聲明):

FONO lofi's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • We only use Google user data to provide user-facing features (account authentication and playlist syncing).
  • We do not sell, rent, or transfer Google user data to third parties, data brokers, or advertising networks.
  • We do not use Google user data for serving advertisements (including personalized, re-targeted, or interest-based ads).
  • We do not allow humans to read user data unless required by applicable law or to resolve security issues with user consent.

2. Strict Policy on AI & Non-Consensual Intimate Imagery (嚴格禁止 AI 非自願親密影像政策)

FONO lofi is strictly compliant with the Google APIs Terms of Service and applicable international digital safety standards:

  • No Generative Image / AI NCII: FONO lofi does not generate, synthesize, modify, host, or distribute Non-Consensual Intimate Imagery (NCII), deepfake adult content, or synthetic depictions of individuals.
  • No Biometric or Facial Processing: FONO lofi does not use Google APIs or machine learning models for facial recognition, facial synthesis, nude generation, or avatar manipulation.
  • Audio Only: Any algorithmic features in FONO lofi (such as the "Mood DJ" or focus presets) operate strictly on musical metadata (genre, tempo, ambient noise levels) to generate ambient playlists.

3. General Information Collected & Local Storage (一般資料收集與本機儲存)

  • Local Audio Vault: Audio tracks you upload to your private vault remain stored directly in your sandboxed browser storage. FONO lofi does not broadcast your private audio files to public feeds.
  • Playback Preferences: Volume levels, selected soundscapes (rain, cafe noise), theme mode, and timers are stored locally in your browser's localStorage.
  • Server Access Logs: Basic technical logs (IP address, user agent, timestamps) collected by our hosting provider (Vercel) for cybersecurity and DDoS mitigation.

4. Data Retention and Deletion Mechanism (使用者資料保留、撤銷與刪除機制)

You have complete sovereignty over your information and can remove your data at any time through three direct mechanisms:

  1. In-App Deletion: Open the in-app Settings menu and click Reset to Defaults or Sign Out. This immediately purges all locally stored playlists, uploaded audio caches, and active session tokens.
  2. Revoke Google OAuth Access: You can disconnect FONO lofi from your Google Account at any time through the official Google Account Permissions manager.
  3. Direct Deletion Request: You can email us at support@fono-lofi.vercel.app with your account email address. We permanently remove any account records from our system within 24 to 48 hours.

5. Security (資訊安全)

All communications between your device, FONO lofi, and Google APIs are encrypted using industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS). We do not store raw Google account passwords.

6. Children's Privacy (兒童隱私保護)

FONO lofi is designed for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 13.
