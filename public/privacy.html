1. Scope & Ungated Public Access (服務範圍與完全公開原則)

1.1 Public Open Access: FONO lofi is designed first and foremost as an ungated web utility. Any individual may visit https://fono-lofi.vercel.app/ and immediately stream curated lo-fi instrumental tracks, configure ambient soundscapes (rain, coffee shop noise, vinyl crackle), and use productivity timers (Pomodoro sessions) without registering, signing up, or providing an email address.

1.2 Voluntary Account Authentication: Creating an account or signing in via third-party identity providers (Google OAuth 2.0 or Sign in with Apple) is 100% voluntary. It is exclusively offered for users who wish to synchronize their custom playlists, liked tracks, and personal audio vault across multiple devices or web browsers.

2. Google User Data: Requested Scopes & Exact Purpose (Google 使用者資料收集與明確用途)

When you voluntarily click "Sign in with Google", FONO lofi requests only the absolute minimum, non-sensitive OpenID Connect identity scopes necessary to authenticate your listening session. We do not request access to Google Drive, Gmail, Google Contacts, Google Calendar, YouTube management, or any restricted storage services.

Google API Scope Data Accessed (資料項目) Exact Purpose & Processing Workflow (明確用途與處理方式)
openid Unique Google Subject ID (sub identifier) Establishes a cryptographically secure OpenID Connect session. Serves as your unique internal identifier to bind your personal playlist configurations to your profile without exposing raw passwords.
https://www.googleapis.com/auth/userinfo.email Email Address & Verification Status Used strictly as your primary account identifier and login handle. Enables playlist retrieval across different devices and allows you to submit account recovery or data deletion requests.
https://www.googleapis.com/auth/userinfo.profile Full Name, Given Name, and Avatar Image URL Used solely to personalize your in-app studio greeting (e.g., displaying "Welcome back, Alex") and showing your user avatar in the top-right listening room header. This information is private to your session and never broadcast to public directories.

FONO lofi strictly complies with the Google APIs Terms of Service and international laws regarding artificial intelligence safety and dignity protection:

FONO lofi strictly prohibits, does not support, and does not provide tools for the creation, generation, synthesis, alteration, distribution, or facilitation of Non-Consensual Intimate Imagery (NCII), synthetic sexually explicit depictions, deepfake pornography, or unauthorized digital likeness transformations.

5. Local Storage, Cookies & Private Audio Vault (本機儲存與個人音樂保險庫)

FONO lofi prioritizes client-side data privacy to ensure maximum user autonomy:

  • Local Preferences (HTML5 localStorage): Your visual theme selection (obsidian dark, cream light, latte), audio volume balances, equalizer presets, and focus timer configurations are stored entirely inside your local browser's sandboxed storage.
  • Private Audio Vault: When you upload custom audio files (MP3, WAV, FLAC) into your library vault, the audio binary data is stored locally within your sandboxed browser storage. FONO lofi does not copy, index, or distribute your private audio files to third parties.
  • No Invasive Tracking Cookies: FONO lofi does not deploy third-party advertising trackers, cross-site behavioral cookies, or canvas fingerprinting scripts.

7. User Rights, Retention & Data Deletion (資料保留、撤銷與刪除權利)

FONO lofi provides instantaneous, user-controlled mechanisms to access, modify, or permanently delete any stored profile information:

Three Complete Methods to Delete or Revoke Your Data:

  1. Instant In-App Reset: Navigate to Settings (設定) → click Reset to Defaults (重設所有資料) or Sign Out (登出). This immediately purges all local cached session tokens, user profiles, playlists, and audio vault indices from your browser.
  2. Revoke Google Account Access: You can disconnect FONO lofi from your Google Account at any time by visiting your official Google Account Permissions Manager. Upon revocation, our app is immediately prohibited from receiving refreshed authentication tokens.
  3. Direct Deletion SLA (24–48 Hours): You can submit a written erasure request to our compliance officer at support@fono-lofi.vercel.app with the subject line "Data Deletion Request". All corresponding server logs and cached identifiers will be permanently destroyed within 48 business hours.

8. Legal Bases for Processing under GDPR & PDPO (處理個人資料之合法依據)

For individuals residing in the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom, or Hong Kong SAR, we process your personal data under the following lawful bases:

  • Consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)): When you voluntarily click "Sign in with Google" or "Sign in with Apple", you explicitly consent to our processing of your name and email address to authenticate your session. You may withdraw this consent at any time.
  • Contractual Performance (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)): To deliver requested features, such as restoring your saved playlists when you log in on a new device.
  • Legitimate Interests (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)): To maintain web infrastructure security, prevent automated bot abuse, and ensure high system availability via standard server access logs.

10. Data Security & Encryption Standards (資訊安全與防護措施)

We employ multi-layered technical and organizational safeguards to protect your information:

  • TLS 1.3 Cryptographic Transit: All data transmitted between your web browser, our Vercel edge endpoints, and Google API servers is strictly encrypted using modern Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS).
  • Zero Plaintext Credential Storage: We do not store raw Google account passwords, credit card numbers, or sensitive security credentials. Identity authentication is performed through ephemeral zero-trust tokens.
  • Isolated Client Sandboxing: User-uploaded files remain sandboxed in browser origin storage (IndexedDB/localStorage) protected by the browser's Same-Origin Policy.

11. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)
