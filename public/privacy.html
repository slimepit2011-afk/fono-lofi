Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 21, 2026 · Application: fono lofi (https://fono-lofi.vercel.app)

At FONO lofi ("we", "our", or "us"), your privacy and trust are our top priorities. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our web application located at https://fono-lofi.vercel.app.

1. Information We Collect

We believe in minimal data collection. We only collect the minimal information necessary to deliver your personalized analog listening experience:

  • Account Information (Google OAuth & Apple ID): When you choose to sign in or register with Google or Apple, we receive your basic public profile details provided by the identity provider: your display name, email address, and profile picture avatar.
  • Custom Music & Playlists: Audio tracks you upload to your private vault, custom playlist names, liked songs, and listening history are stored locally in your browser to maintain your unique personal listening library.
  • Technical Information: Standard browser request headers, IP address, and cookie/session identifiers necessary to deliver pages securely over HTTPS.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information exclusively for the following purposes:

  • To identify your account and authenticate your access to FONO lofi.
  • To persist your personal uploaded audio files, custom playlists, and loved tracks.
  • To calculate listening analytics and focus streaks displayed in your personal stats dashboard.
  • To maintain app security, prevent abuse, and ensure high playback performance.
Google API Services User Data Policy Compliance:
FONO lofi's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not sell your Google user data, and we do not use your data for advertising or marketing.

3. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, trade, or monetize your personal data. We only share information with third-party service providers strictly required to operate the application:

  • Google Identity Services: To facilitate secure OAuth 2.0 authentication.
  • Apple ID Services: To facilitate Sign in with Apple authentication.
  • Vercel Inc.: Our hosting and content delivery network provider.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

You have complete control over your data:

  • You can delete your custom uploaded songs and playlists at any time inside the app.
  • You can disconnect your Google or Apple account and wipe your session directly in Settings or by clearing your browser cache.
  • To request permanent deletion of any server-side logs associated with your account, please email us at support@fono-lofi.vercel.app.

5. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures, including HTTPS/TLS encryption for all data in transit and sandboxed browser storage. We never store raw passwords for third-party OAuth providers.

6. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted directly on this page with an updated effective date.

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy or your data, please contact us at:
