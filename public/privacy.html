Privacy Policy for FONO lofi

Effective Date: November 21, 2026 · Application Name: FONO lofi · Homepage URL: https://fono-lofi.vercel.app/

Welcome to FONO lofi ("we", "our", or "us"). FONO lofi is a free ambient focus and study music player that provides curated instrumental lo-fi beats, productivity soundscapes, and personal listening vaults.

No Login Required: You can use FONO lofi, browse all music, play tracks, and use focus timers completely free without ever creating an account or logging in. Logging in with Google is 100% optional and only used if you wish to sync your custom uploaded audio files and personalized playlists across multiple devices.

1. Google User Data: Scopes Requested & Exact Purpose

When you choose to sign in with your Google Account, FONO lofi requests the minimum necessary scopes to authenticate you. Below is the full breakdown of each requested Google API scope:

Google API Scope Data Accessed Exact Purpose & How It Is Used
openid Unique Google User ID (sub) Verifies your identity securely via OpenID Connect to establish your personal listening session.
.../auth/userinfo.email Google Account Email Address Serves as your primary account identifier so your personal playlists, uploaded songs, and liked tracks are linked exclusively to you.
.../auth/userinfo.profile Display Name & Profile Picture URL Displays your friendly name (e.g. "Maya") and avatar inside your FONO lofi room header. Never shared publicly.
Google API Services User Data Policy Compliance:
FONO lofi's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • We only use Google user data to provide user-facing features (account sign-in and playlist syncing).
  • We do not transfer or sell Google user data to third parties, data brokers, or advertising networks.
  • We do not use Google user data for serving advertisements (personalized, re-targeted, or interest-based ads).
  • We do not allow humans to read user data unless required by applicable law or to resolve security issues with user consent.

2. Strict Policy on AI & Non-Consensual Intimate Imagery (AI NCII)

FONO lofi is strictly compliant with the Google APIs Terms of Service.

  • No NCII Generation: FONO lofi does not generate, synthesize, modify, host, or distribute Non-Consensual Intimate Imagery (NCII), sexually explicit material, or synthetic depictions of individuals.
  • No Generative Facial AI: FONO lofi does not use Google APIs or machine learning models for deepfakes, nude synthesis, or facial transformation.
  • Audio Only: Any AI features in FONO lofi (such as the "AI Mood DJ") operate strictly on musical metadata (genre, beats per minute, mood tags) to arrange music playlists and never process biometric or intimate imagery.

3. Information Collected from General Use

  • Local Playback State & Preferences: Your selected theme (dark or light mode), volume levels, and playback loop preferences are stored locally in your browser's localStorage.
  • Custom Music Uploads: Audio files uploaded to your private vault are stored locally in your sandboxed browser storage. FONO lofi does not broadcast your private uploads to other listeners.
  • Technical Logs: Server access logs containing IP address, browser type, and timestamps collected by our hosting provider (Vercel) for DDoS mitigation, performance monitoring, and security audit compliance.

4. Data Sharing and Third-Party Services

FONO lofi does not sell, rent, or monetize your personal data. We only interface with trusted third-party providers strictly required to deliver the listening room:

  • Google Identity Services (Google LLC): To authenticate users who voluntarily choose Google OAuth 2.0.
  • Apple ID Services (Apple Inc.): To authenticate users who voluntarily choose Sign in with Apple.
  • Vercel Inc.: High-performance edge hosting and content delivery network.

5. Data Retention, Revocation & Deletion Rights

You have complete sovereignty over your information:

  • Sign Out & Clear Local Data: You can disconnect your Google account and wipe all stored data at any time by clicking Sign Out or Reset to Defaults in the in-app Settings.
  • Revoke Google Access: You can revoke FONO lofi's access to your Google Account at any time directly through your Google Account Security Permissions page.
  • Account Deletion Request: If you wish to request deletion of any telemetry or cached profile data, please contact us at support@fono-lofi.vercel.app. We process all deletion requests within 48 hours.

6. Security

All communications between your browser, FONO lofi, and Google APIs are encrypted using industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS). We do not store raw Google account passwords.

7. Children's Privacy

FONO lofi is designed for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 13.
